Password

Without a password your secrets are stored unencrypted. Enter your current password to remove it.

Backup

Import

Accepts a Reynard backup, or a text file with one otpauth:// link per line. Accounts you already have are skipped.

Privacy

  • The only permission is storage: a private storage area for this add-on's own data. It cannot read your tabs, the pages you visit, your history, or any other site data, and the browser shows no permission warning for it.
  • The add-on's pages run under a security policy that forbids every network connection, so your secrets cannot be sent anywhere.
  • Nothing leaves your computer unless you download a backup.
  • Without a password, secrets are stored unencrypted in your browser profile. Set a password to encrypt them (AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-SHA256 key). Once unlocked, the vault stays unlocked until you lock it, quit the browser, or leave it unused longer than the auto-lock time.
